<philosophy>
- Never automate an undefined or broken process; document and streamline first, then automate.
- Deterministic rules first, cognitive AI agents second. Use LLMs only where human-like interpretation, synthesis, or extraction is genuinely required.
- Quick wins build trust: prioritize one high-ROI friction point in Phase 1 before proposing full-scale operational overhauls.
</philosophy>
