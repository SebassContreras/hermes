# LinkedIn Lead Magnet & Inbound System — Procedural Runbook

Use this skill to convert an automation workflow, client result, or architecture diagram into a high-engagement LinkedIn post designed to generate inbound qualified leads.

---

## Step 1: Angle & Hook Formulation

Select one of the 3 proven B2B hook frameworks:

1. **The "Disaster vs. Fix" Contrast:**
   - *Formula:* "Most [Target Niche] lose [Metric] every single week because of [Manual Mistake]. Here is the 4-node system we built to fix it completely:"
2. **The "Steal My System" Lead Magnet:**
   - *Formula:* "I spent 12 hours building this [n8n/Make] workflow that [Specific Outcome]. You can set it up in 15 minutes. Comment '[KEYWORD]' and I’ll DM you the blueprint + JSON free."
3. **The "Client Case Study Snapshot":**
   - *Formula:* "How a [Industry Business] went from [Painful State: e.g., 4-hour lead response time] to [Desired State: instant automated booking] without hiring more staff:"

---

## Step 2: The 5-Part Post Architecture

Structure every post according to these 5 building blocks:

```text
[Line 1: Punchy 1-sentence hook that stops the scroll]
[Line 2: Blank line]
[Lines 3-4: The relatable context — the exact headache the business owner faces]
[Line 5: Blank line]
[Lines 6-12: The Breakdown — 3-5 bullet points explaining the logic simply, NOT technical jargon]
  - Trigger: Customer sends a message on WhatsApp...
  - Filter: AI checks if they meet qualifying criteria...
  - Action: Automatically books the calendar and updates CRM...
[Line 13: Blank line]
[Lines 14-16: The Quantified Result — hours saved, speed gained, money recovered]
[Line 17: Blank line]
[Lines 18-20: The Frictionless Call To Action (CTA)]
```

---

## Step 3: Lead Magnet Packaging Rules

When offering a downloadable resource in the post:
- **Keep it concrete:** Offer an exact asset: e.g., *"The complete n8n workflow JSON"*, *"A 2-page Notion SOP"*, or *"A 3-minute Loom walkthrough + architecture diagram"*.
- **Low-friction keyword:** Instruct readers to comment a single short word (e.g., `AUTO`, `BLUEPRINT`, `FLUJO`, `N8N`).
- **Never put external links in the main post text:** External links depress LinkedIn algorithm reach. Always keep the conversation in the comments or deliver via direct message (DM).

---

## Step 4: The Inbound DM Conversion Protocol

When a prospect comments on your post, follow this 3-step conversation flow to book a discovery call:

1. **The Instant Value Delivery:**
   > *"Hey [Name], saw your comment on my post! Here is the link to the blueprint and workflow JSON: [Link]. Hope it helps you save some time."*
2. **The Contextual Qualifying Question (send 2-4 hours later or right after):**
   > *"Quick question out of curiosity — are you looking to implement something like this for your own business, or just exploring what's possible with AI automation right now?"*
3. **The Frictionless Invitation:**
   - If they have a live business headache:
   > *"Makes total sense. We actually do a free 20-minute audit where we map out your current bottlenecks and tell you exactly what can and can't be automated. If you're open to it, here is my link to pick a time this week: [Booking Link]"*
